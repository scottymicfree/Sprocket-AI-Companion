# FENTON LAB
### Our Story — Version 1.0

**Independent Thinking. Intelligent Software. Human Control.**

## Every great idea starts somewhere.

Fenton Lab didn't begin in a corporate office, with a large engineering team or millions of dollars in funding.

It began at home in Missouri, with a personal computer, an ambitious idea, and a determination to build something different.

Founded by Randy Webb, Fenton Lab is an emerging independent software engineering company focused on creating intelligent applications that feel personal, useful, and genuinely connected to the people using them.

Our starting question was simple:

**What if technology could become more capable without taking control away from the person using it?**

That question grew into our first major undertaking: **Lucy AIOS**.

## The Beginning — Lucy AIOS

Lucy began as an ambitious vision for an intelligent operating environment.

Rather than creating another chatbot, we wanted to explore software capable of understanding context, coordinating specialized agents, interacting with computer systems, and operating within verifiable boundaries.

Lucy introduced the principles that continue to guide Fenton Lab:

- Intelligence should operate with accountable permissions.
- User information should remain under the user's control.
- Software should distinguish evidence from assumptions.
- AI should assist human decisions rather than silently replace them.
- Powerful capabilities should not require surrendering privacy.

Lucy grew into a substantial engineering project involving governance, memory, specialized operating modes, verification systems, and thousands of development tests.

But building ambitious software also means confronting practical limitations.

Hardware failures and resource constraints eventually slowed Lucy's development.

We could have stopped there.

Instead, we changed direction without abandoning the vision.

## Meet Sprocket — A Little Fox With a Big Curiosity

Sprocket started with a different question:

**What if an AI companion could feel like a living part of your desktop?**

Not just another window containing a chatbot, but an expressive character that moves, reacts, remembers conversations, and helps you with your work.

Sprocket became our first product-focused development effort.

She is an animated fox designed to live on a Windows desktop, combining personality, local memory, optional AI conversation, and carefully controlled interactions.

Behind the character is the same philosophy that inspired Lucy: useful capabilities, clear permissions, and respect for the user.

Our intention is to make Sprocket approachable, enjoyable, and affordable, with ongoing character improvements that reward ownership rather than requiring a new purchase for every small evolution.

Sprocket is progressing through pre-release development and validation.

## Sprocket Family — Making Learning Feel Alive

As Sprocket developed, another opportunity emerged.

What if the same expressive character could help children enjoy learning?

That idea became **Sprocket Family**, an educational companion being developed for children ages 7–12.

With parent-controlled profiles, reading, mathematics, science, beginner coding, and guided homework activities, Sprocket Family aims to make learning feel like something children experience with a companion rather than something they complete alone.

Its early prototypes already include animated teaching reactions, offline educational activities, progress tracking, parental controls, and a modular Learning Library.

Our longer-term vision is to give parents the ability to choose educational packages and create personalized study plans without relying on constant application updates.

The goal isn't to replace teachers or parents.

It's to give children another engaging way to explore, practice, and build confidence.

## Liberty — A Different Kind of Companion

Our next character, Liberty, brings a different personality and purpose.

A proud American bald eagle, Liberty is being developed as a calm, observant desktop research companion.

His proposed signature capability, **Patriot Lens**, is designed around a simple interaction:

Select something on the screen. Research it. Understand it. Decide what to do next.

Whether a user is studying code, examining a technical document, reading unfamiliar material, or investigating an idea, Liberty's goal is to connect the selected information with relevant conversation context and offer practical assistance.

Research This. Explain This. Debug or Improve. Teach Me About This.

These capabilities are under development, but they reflect a broader direction for Fenton Lab: intelligent companions with distinct identities, specialized purposes, and reusable engineering foundations.

## Built Differently. Built With Purpose.

We're building Fenton Lab around several principles.

**Local-first where practical.** Users should be able to benefit from software without unnecessarily transferring personal information to remote services.

**Permission before action.** An intelligent assistant should not assume that access to a computer grants permission to control it.

**Personality with purpose.** An expressive character should offer more than entertainment. Its personality should make the software more approachable and useful.

**Engineering backed by evidence.** Features should be tested, failures documented, and limitations acknowledged.

**Ownership and long-term value.** We want products people can purchase, enjoy, and continue improving with, rather than experiences defined entirely by recurring subscription fees.

These principles are aspirations we continually work to implement and verify—not claims that every planned feature is already complete.

## Where We Are Today

Fenton Lab is still at the beginning of its business journey.

We're preparing our first products for commercial release, building our public presence, refining our software, and working through the practical requirements of starting an independent software company.

We don't have the resources of a major technology corporation.

What we do have is a growing body of real engineering work, functioning prototypes, a clear direction, and a willingness to keep building even when the path becomes difficult.

Our projects are at different stages of development, and none should be mistaken for a certified or publicly available commercial product until its release requirements are complete.

## The Future of Fenton Lab

Our long-term vision is a family of intelligent software experiences built on shared engineering but designed for different needs.

Lucy represents intelligent coordination and governed computing.

Sprocket represents personality, everyday companionship, and approachable assistance.

Sprocket Family represents interactive learning.

Liberty represents investigation, understanding, and research.

Different products. Different personalities. One underlying philosophy.

**Technology should work for people—not the other way around.**

We're not trying to build another collection of disposable applications.

We're trying to build software people genuinely want to keep.

And we're only getting started.
